Fan replacement

Diagnose failing fans and perform a step-by-step replacement.

Home • Tutorials

Overview

Learn how to identify a failing fan (noisy, intermittent, or not spinning), source a compatible replacement, and replace it safely.

Tools

  • Screwdriver set
  • Compressed air and brush
  • Replacement fan compatible with your model

Steps

  1. Power down, unplug, and remove the battery if possible.
  2. Open the case and identify the fan assembly.
  3. Disconnect the fan cable from the motherboard carefully.
  4. Remove mounting screws and extract the old fan; install the new fan and secure the screws.
  5. Reconnect the fan cable and test before fully reassembling the case.
