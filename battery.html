Battery replacement

Safely remove and replace internal and external batteries; check battery health.

Home • Tutorials

Overview

This tutorial covers identifying battery type (removable vs internal), safe removal and installation, battery health diagnostics, and disposal/recycling best practices.

Tools

  • Screwdriver set (model dependent)
  • Plastic spudger
  • Anti-static wrist strap
  • Multimeter (for diagnostic checks)

Steps

  1. Back up data and power down the laptop.
  2. Unplug charger and remove any external battery if present.
  3. Open the case following the service manual. Keep screws organized.
  4. Locate the battery pack and inspect connector/cable for damage.
  5. Disconnect the battery cable from the motherboard before removing the pack.
  6. Remove mounting screws or adhesive, then swap the battery.
  7. Reconnect the battery cable, reassemble, and charge the battery fully. Observe charging behavior.
  8. Run battery health checks (OS-provided diagnostics or third-party tools) to confirm expected capacity and cycle count.

When to seek help

  • Battery is swollen — stop immediately and seek professional disposal.
  • Battery connector or motherboard power circuit shows damage or corrosion.
