BIOS/firmware & reset

Update BIOS safely, recover from bad updates, and perform NVRAM/CMOS resets.

Home • Tutorials

Overview

This guide describes when to update firmware, how to prepare for updates, and how to recover if an update fails.

Precautions

  • Only use firmware from the manufacturer for your exact model.
  • Ensure the battery is charged and the laptop is connected to reliable power during updates.
  • Follow the manufacturer's update instructions precisely.

Recovery steps

  1. If the system fails to boot after an update, attempt a CMOS reset (consult your model's manual for the jumper or battery removal steps).
  2. Some manufacturers provide BIOS recovery tools or USB recovery images — follow their documented process.
  3. If the motherboard is unresponsive, seek professional repair; firmware recovery can be hardware-specific.
