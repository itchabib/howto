RAM upgrades

  • Check your laptop's maximum supported RAM and the type (DDR4/DDR5) before buying.
  • Some laptops have soldered RAM and cannot be upgraded; check the service manual.
  • When installing, align the module at an angle and press down until it clicks into the retention clips.

Storage upgrades (HDD/SSD)

  • Consider NVMe M.2 SSDs for significant speed improvements if your laptop supports them.
  • Clone your drive or reinstall the OS after installing a new drive.
  • Keep mounting screws and brackets — many laptops share the same screw sizes across models.
