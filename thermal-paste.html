When to reapply thermal paste

If your laptop runs hotter than usual even after cleaning, or if it's several years old, replacing thermal paste can improve temperatures.

Procedure

  1. Power off and remove power sources.
  2. Remove heatsink carefully; consult model guide for screw order.
  3. Wipe old thermal compound from CPU/GPU and heatsink with lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol.
  4. Apply a pea-sized drop of new thermal paste to the center of the CPU die.
  5. Reattach heatsink, tightening screws in a criss-cross pattern to distribute pressure evenly.
  6. Reassemble and test temperatures under load (e.g., run a stress test and monitor with hwmonitor or similar tool).
