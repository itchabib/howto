Cleaning fans and vents

  1. Power off, unplug, and remove battery if possible.
  2. Open the case following the model-specific service manual.
  3. Hold fan blades still and blow compressed air through the fan and vents in short bursts from outside and inside.
  4. Use a soft brush to remove stubborn dust from fins of the heatsink.
  5. Inspect for hair or debris caught in the fan hub and remove carefully.

When to replace a fan

  • Fan makes grinding or rattling noises when powered on.
  • Fan fails to spin or spins intermittently.
  • Excessive heat after cleaning suggests failing fan or heatsink contact.
